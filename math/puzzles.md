
## Brothers and Sisters
```
A boy says: "I have twice as many sisters as brothers."
His sister says: "I have the same number of brothers as sisters."
How many boys and girls are in the family?
```
## The Chickens and Rabbits
```
A farmer has 35 animals consisting of chickens and rabbits.
Together they have 94 legs.
How many chickens and rabbits are there?
```

## The Bicycle Journey
```
Two cyclists start from towns 90 km apart and ride toward each other.
The first cyclist travels at 15 km/h, and the second at 10 km/h.
A dog runs back and forth between them at 20 km/h until the cyclists meet.
How far does the dog run?
```

## The Clock Hands
```
At exactly 3:00, the hands of a clock form a right angle.
At what time after 3:00 will the hands overlap for the first time?
Give your answer in minutes and seconds.
```

## The Chocolate Bar
```
A chocolate bar originally costs $10.
Its price increases by x percent, then decreases by the same x percent.
The final price is $9.60.
What is x?
```
