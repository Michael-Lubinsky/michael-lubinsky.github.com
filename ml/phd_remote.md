For your interests in AI, Data Science, Mathematics, and Physics, I would focus on research-oriented PhD programs from accredited universities, rather than simply looking for the easiest online doctorate.

There is an important difference between these fields:

- AI / Computer Science: Several universities offer online PhD programs, including programs with dissertations.
- Data Science: Online doctorates exist, but some are more focused on professional applications than original research.
- Mathematics: Fully online, research-oriented PhD programs are much harder to find.
- Physics: Fully online PhD programs are exceptionally rare, particularly in theoretical physics.

Since you are interested in mathematical and computational research, I would prioritize programs that allow a dissertation involving machine learning theory, causal inference, mathematical modeling, or computational physics.

University of South Africa (UNISA) — PhD Mathematics / Physics

UNISA lists separate PhD programs in Mathematics and Physics. Its Mathematics doctorate is registered in South Africa's national qualifications framework and centers on a research thesis.

UNISA specializes in distance education, but you should verify whether your specific research project and examinations can be completed entirely from the United States.

A PhD in Physics Education or Mathematics Education is different from a PhD in Physics or Mathematics. If your interest is quantum mechanics, mathematical physics, or theoretical mathematics, you should look for the latter.
